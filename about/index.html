About Amrutha Reddy Konala

Strategic advisor, fractional CMO, creative director and author of Structural Marketing: The 9 Pillar Operating System.

01. Profile

Amrutha Reddy Konala is a strategic advisor and the author of Structural Marketing: The 9 Pillar Operating System (2026), a 10-volume, 4,614-page treatment of marketing as an architectural engineering discipline rather than a promotional one.

Her advisory work has spanned heritage and family enterprises, real estate developments, private principals and political campaigns. Structural market failures recur in the same shapes across very different industries, which is what makes a structural method portable.

With the research and the books complete, she now also works as a fractional CMO: taking the whole marketing system of a growing company (competition, strategy, customer, value, pricing, channels, timing, execution and measurement) and running it as one structure, reported in the language of the P&L.

She is also a creative director and 2D animator: screenplay, character design, illustration, animation and brand design, so a position can be carried all the way to finished film and design.

She leads a small team for writing, design, video and distribution, so advice can be carried through to finished work. Everyone on the team works under the same confidentiality she does.

04. Honours

Noble court title

Kanjeng Raden Ajeng Tumenggung (KRAjT)

Kingdom of Mangkualaman, Java, Indonesia • Conferred 24 July 2026

Register 043/2026

On 24 July 2026, His Majesty Kangjeng Gusti Pangeran Adipati Aria Mangku Alam II of the Kingdom of Mangkualaman conferred on her the permanent noble title Kanjeng Raden Ajeng Tumenggung (KRAjT), entered on the court register under reference 043/SM/KRAjT/MA/GC/2026.

In Javanese court usage, Tumenggung is a senior rank, historically the level at which a court's affairs were administered. It was conferred on her as a foreign professional for strategic advisory work and for establishing the kingdom's global digital presence.
