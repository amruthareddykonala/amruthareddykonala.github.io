Volume III • Definitive Edition • 470 pages • 175,000 Words

Structural Marketing: WHO

Customer unit economics, psychographic resonance, demand quality

By Amrutha Reddy Konala

"Growth that degrades your gross margin is not expansion; it is structural decay disguised as volume. Choose your customers with the same rigor you choose your board."

Volume III dismantles generic buyer personas in favor of rigorous customer unit economics, psychological architecture, and demand quality indexation.

Examines how different customer cohorts either reinforce or erode enterprise operating models, and how to calibrate acquisition around high-margin, low-friction demand.

Covers multi-stakeholder buying committee dynamics, deep psychographic resonance profiles, and the economics of customer qualification.

What the volume covers

  • Customer unit economics and the Demand Quality Index (DQI)
  • Deep psychographic resonance and psychological architecture mapping
  • Buying committee power topography and multi-stakeholder dynamics
  • Structural customer disqualifiers and non-customer governance

Excerpt

The fatal temptation of growth-stage companies is accepting any customer willing to write a cheque. In doing so, they introduce toxic customization requests, distort their product roadmap, and burden their customer success teams with negative-margin support burdens. Structural Marketing mandates strict customer refusal gates.
