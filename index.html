Marketing, built as a structure, not a campaign.

"Strategy that cannot be built is merely a recommendation."

Amrutha Reddy Konala works with founders, family enterprises and leadership teams as a strategic advisor and fractional CMO. Her method is Structural Marketing: The 9 Pillar Operating System, a 10-volume body of work written over eight years: nine pillars, nine questions, from competition to measurement, answered as one system and priced in the language of the P&L. She works with a small team for writing, design, video and distribution.

The practice

One practitioner. Five disciplines. One unbroken architecture.

Most of the marketing industry is organised around the gap between strategy and execution: one agency decides the position, another writes it, another designs it, another animates it, another codes it, and the architecture leaks at every handover.

01

Long-form structural writing, and the narrative architecture underneath a position.

A 4,614-page, 1.74-million-word treatment of marketing as an architectural discipline written over eight years, alongside researched historical non-fiction, a co-authored work with a former Union Cabinet Minister, and three original IP universes. A position that cannot be told as a coherent story is a position that has not been settled.

Design and Brand Systems

02

Identity, typographic and editorial systems built as systems, not as disposable artefacts.

Visual identity, mark and typographic systems, editorial layout, and the presentation layer of a brand, designed so the rules survive the designer. Heritage and legacy work carries an additional constraint: the system must look as though it was always there.

  • Identity Systems
  • Typographic Systems
  • Editorial & Print Design
  • Heraldic & Noble Insignia
  • Design Tokens

2D and 3D Animation

03

Character rigging, deformation and motion built to production rigs rather than one-off files.

2D character animation and rigging in Toon Boom Harmony (deformers, cut-out rigs, master controllers) and 3D work in Blender, including node-based procedural rigging. Motion is treated as a manufacturing problem: a rig that only its author can operate has not been finished.

  • Toon Boom Harmony Rigs
  • Blender 3D Modeling
  • Master Controllers
  • Cut-Out Deformation
  • Storyboard to Final Film

Software and Automation

04

Working software, including the tools the advisory work runs on.

Applications, automation pipelines and command-line tooling built in Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Flutter, Firebase and C. She is now building the Marketing Operating System (MOS), software, separate from the books, that turns the nine pillars of Structural Marketing: The 9 Pillar Operating System into a working operating process for client teams.

Eight years of research

Structural Marketing:
The 9 Pillar Operating System

A 4,614-page, 1.74-million-word work in ten volumes (COMPETE, WHY, WHO, WHAT, HOW MUCH, WHERE, WHEN, HOW, SO WHAT and THE SYSTEMS) that treats marketing as an engineering discipline of loads, failure modes and defensible moats.

Explore the 10 volumes

The ten volumes

  • 01 COMPETE: Power dynamics, game theory, moat physics
  • 02 WHY: Exclusionary strategy, commitment, trade-off geometry
  • 03 WHO: Customer unit economics, psychographic resonance, demand quality
  • 04 WHAT: Value contract, proof credibility, semiotic signaling
  • 05 HOW MUCH: Pricing authority, monetization architecture, revenue model design
  • 06 WHERE: Access governance, algorithmic distribution, channel politics
  • 07 WHEN: Timing windows, sequencing logic, readiness gates
  • 08 HOW: Execution decision architecture, capability maturity, operational debt
  • 09 SO WHAT: Signal integrity, causal inference, feedback velocity
  • 10 THE SYSTEMS: The eleven System Maps and the integrated field
