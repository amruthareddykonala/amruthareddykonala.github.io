Services · Sprint

Sprint

One big problem, diagnosed, designed and built with your team in three to six weeks. For when you already know what is wrong and need it fixed, not studied.

How it runs

  1. Week 1, diagnose: the evidence on the problem, and the real constraint behind it.
  2. Week 2, design: the change, its owners and what it should return, agreed in writing.
  3. Weeks 3–6, build: your team and agencies put it in place, with me in a weekly working session and on call between them.
  4. Hand-over: a short written record of what was built and how to run it.

Any project on the services page can be run as a Sprint.

Ask about a Sprint

Questions

What counts as “one big problem”?+

Something with a clear outcome and an owner: repositioning before a launch, fixing a channel that has stopped paying back, rebuilding pricing and discounts, setting up measurement your board trusts. If it needs more than one, we split it into two Sprints or use the Fractional CMO role.

How is a Sprint different from a project?+

A project diagnoses and designs one area. A Sprint does that and then builds it with your team, so you finish with the change in place, not just a plan.

Who does the building?+

Your team and your agencies, with me alongside every week. Where a Sprint needs writing, design or video, my small team can do that part.
