How every engagement runs

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    I read your numbers, documents and channels, talk to the people who run them, and write down what is actually wrong, with the evidence.

  2. 02

    Design

    We decide what to change and what to stop. You get the choices in writing, with what each costs and what it should return.

  3. 03

    Build

    Your team or agencies put it in place with me alongside, in a Sprint or a project’s build phase, against agreed owners and dates.

  4. 04

    Govern

    It runs, gets measured and gets reported, monthly or quarterly. This is the work of the Fractional CMO role.

Questions

Which should I start with?+

If you are unsure, book the strategy session. You leave with a written note on what to fix first, whether or not we work together again. If you want the whole picture, start with the Marketing Audit.

Why fixed prices, and why no discounts?+

The price follows your company’s size band, so every client in a band pays the same. If the budget is smaller, I offer a smaller fixed scope, never a lower price for the same work.

Why payment in advance?+

It lets me reserve the time and start on the agreed date. Every engagement has a written scope and timeline before you pay. Projects are paid half at booking and half at delivery; the strategy session, the Audit and the Fractional CMO retainer are paid in advance.

Why are there no case studies?+

My clients’ work is confidential, and yours will be too. I share client names and sectors publicly, and a private reference pack on request.

What is the difference between the Marketing Audit and a project?+

The Audit covers the whole marketing system, all nine pillars, and tells you where to act first. A project goes deep on one area. Many clients start with the Audit and then take one or two projects.

Do you execute, or only advise?+

I diagnose, design and oversee. Execution stays with your team or your agencies, and a Sprint or the Fractional CMO role carries the work through with them. A small team works with me on writing, design, video and distribution where a project needs it.

Do you work with companies like ours?+

Owner-led and mid-size companies of roughly ₹50–1,000 Cr revenue, in India and abroad, across sectors: founder-led and family-owned businesses, heritage and real estate holdings, consumer brands, B2B and services firms.

Is the first call a sales pitch?+

It is a short fit check with a set agenda. If I am not the right fit, I will say so and, where I can, tell you who might be.

Can you help with DPDP?+

I review consent journeys, contact lists and how marketing uses personal data, and give you a DPDP-ready marketing checklist. Legal advice and sign-off stay with your lawyers.

Do you take commissions from agencies or platforms?+

No. I take no commissions, referral fees or rebates from agencies, platforms or vendors, so my recommendations have no side interest.
