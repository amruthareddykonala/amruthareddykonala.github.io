Structural Marketing

Most marketing problems are not creative problems. They are structural ones: a position, a price or a channel that was never designed to carry the load put on it.

Nine questions, one system

Structural Marketing answers nine questions in order, and checks every answer against the others. A weak answer anywhere shows up as a cost somewhere else.

  1. 01

    COMPETE

    Where can we win, and against whom?

  2. 02

    WHY

    What are we choosing, and what are we refusing?

  3. 03

    WHO

    Which customers create value worth serving?

  4. 04

    WHAT

    What do we promise, and how do we prove it?

  5. 05

    HOW MUCH

    How do we price and capture the value we create?

  6. 06

    WHERE

    Where do customers find, buy and use it?

  7. 07

    WHEN

    When do we move, and in what order?

  8. 08

    HOW

    How does the organisation actually execute?

  9. 09

    SO WHAT

    What did it produce, in the language of the P&L?

The tenth volume, THE SYSTEMS, shows how the nine fit together.
